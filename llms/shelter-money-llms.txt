# Shelter

> A read-only AI money guardian that forecasts the next 30 days and explains cash-flow risk before payday.

Last reviewed: 2026-08-23

## Direct answer

Shelter connects to supported financial accounts through Plaid with read-only access. It combines balances, income timing, recurring bills, debt payments, subscriptions, and observed spending to publish a 30-day cash-flow forecast. Guardian uses that forecast to explain what is already spoken for, when the balance is likely to be lowest, and one action the user can consider.

Shelter cannot move, hold, withdraw, or spend money. It does not make payments, cancel subscriptions, or negotiate bills.

## Core capabilities

- Versioned 30-day daily-balance forecast
- Safe-to-spend and before-purchase checks
- Recurring bill and subscription detection
- Predicted low-balance and cash-shortfall alerts
- Debt-payment planning that checks the forecast first
- Guardian chat and proactive updates, including optional Telegram delivery

## Important limitations

- Forecasts are estimates, not guarantees or individualized financial advice.
- New transactions, delayed income, changed due dates, missing accounts, and sync interruptions can change an answer.
- When required money data is missing, stale, or incompatible, Shelter suppresses the forecast-dependent answer instead of treating missing values as zero.
- Users should confirm their current balance and provider terms before making a payment.
